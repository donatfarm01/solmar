An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6 — drive, steal cars, outrun the police and run missions across Solmar City. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty.

Solmar is a 2D top-down open-world game set in the fictional coastal city of Solmar City. You play Danny Reyes, a courier driver taking jobs from fixer Marla Voss: explore eight districts on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge traffic, fight on foot, raise a 0–5 star wanted level and lose the police, and complete six story missions — from a first parcel run at Marina Bay to the harbour score at the South Docks. Everything is original: the city, characters, story, art and the synthesized WebAudio soundtrack are made for this game, with no assets from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, E to enter or steal a car, mouse aim and click to shoot, F to punch, Shift to run, N for the next mission, M to mute, Esc to pause. Progress saves to localStorage.
