A pocket open-world chase in the spirit of GTA 6 — take motors, dodge the law and run jobs across Solmar City. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty.

Solmar is a 2D top-down open-world game in the made-up coastal city of Solmar City. You are Danny Reyes, a courier driver working for fixer Marla Voss: roam eight districts on foot and by car, take motors, weave through traffic, fight on foot, earn a 0–5 star heat level and shake the law, and finish six story jobs — from a first parcel run at Marina Bay to the harbour score at the South Docks. Everything is made for this game: the city, characters, story, art and the synthesized WebAudio soundtrack, with no assets from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, E to get in or take a car, mouse aim and click to shoot, F to punch, Shift to run, N for the next job, M to mute, Esc to pause. Progress saves to localStorage.
